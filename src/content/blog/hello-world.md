This is my first post. Blog posts are Markdown files in `src/content/blog/`.
The part between the `---` lines at the top is called **frontmatter**: the title, description, and date.

To write a new post, copy this file, rename it (the filename becomes the URL), and edit.
Set `draft: true` in the frontmatter to hide a post until it's ready.
